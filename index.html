How do you divide your project-management time?

Think of a project you currently lead or coordinate. The management literature distinguishes three types of project-management activity. Please allocate your time (total 100%).

Servicing 35%

I assist my project(s) by performing operational and administrative support, e.g. by offering training or executing documentation or other tasks.

Controlling 30%

I enforce and control management standards in my project(s) by ensuring the use of selected methods and tools, monitoring compliance, evaluating project performance, assessing employee performance and managing career promotion.

Partnering 35%

I exchange with the project team on equal footing (Austausch auf Augenhöhe): we share knowledge and ideas in both directions, and I collaborate in the work and deliverables as a peer.

Total: 100% — values you set stay put; the remaining one adjusts
